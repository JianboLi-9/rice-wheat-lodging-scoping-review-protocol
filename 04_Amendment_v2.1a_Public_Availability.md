## Clarification

AMEND-2.1 remains valid: OSF registration metadata, identifier and the irreversible Register/Submit/Make Public action may be deferred while final authorship and contributor information are not finalized.

Before formal Review Conduct begins, however, the current protocol must be publicly accessible through a legitimate, stable, project-specific route that does not require authentication. A local D-drive copy, a private link, an expiring share, an unrelated public project or an anonymous paste service does not satisfy this requirement.

The public protocol route is distinct from OSF preregistration. The public route may be an existing authorized GitHub/GitLab repository, an institutional website or another stable authorized repository. The project must record the actual public URL, version/release identifier, publication timestamp, unauthenticated-access verification time and Protocol SHA-256 before formal search execution.

## Non-changes

This clarification changes no review design, research question, Population, Concept, Context, Eligibility, language/year/publication-type rule, database set, WOS-A-001, Stage 2 decision, screening, extraction, robustness, Study Family rule or bibliometric decision. It does not authorize OSF publication, screening, extraction or any security bypass.
