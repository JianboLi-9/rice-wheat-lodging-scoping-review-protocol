## Change made

The timing dependency between OSF public registration and formal Stage 3 evidence identification is changed. Final human authorship, author order, affiliation, ORCID, funding, conflict-of-interest and contributor metadata may be finalized later in the review lifecycle. Therefore, OSF registration/identifier and the irreversible OSF Register/Submit/Make Public action are no longer hard prerequisites for beginning formal database evidence identification under this amendment.

The protocol remains a priori frozen through Protocol v2.0 FINAL plus this prospective amendment. A legitimate public, accessible version of the current protocol must nevertheless be available before formal Review Conduct under the strict JBI/PRISMA reporting boundary. The actual OSF Register/Submit/Make Public action remains a human action and must be recorded with its real date, URL, registration identifier and DOI (if issued). The project must transparently preserve the pre-search amendment date and all old/new hashes.

## Explicit non-changes

This amendment does **not** change:

- review design;
- research questions;
- Population, Concept or Context;
- Eligibility Criteria;
- English + Chinese language eligibility;
- no-lower-publication-year rule;
- publication-type eligibility;
- prespecified database set: WoS Core Collection, IEEE Xplore and CNKI, with supplementary backward/forward citation searching;
- frozen WOS-A-001 query;
- Stage 2 search-strategy decisions;
- screening design, two-human-reviewer requirement, 25-record pilot or >=75% agreement threshold;
- data-extraction or Study Family rules;
- non-exclusionary robustness mapping;
- bibliometric pending decisions;
- formal review conduct boundaries other than the registration-timing dependency stated above.

## Operational consequence

HG-01 is closed from human-supplied IEEE evidence. HG-02 (OSF metadata) and HG-03 (human OSF public action) remain OPEN/DEFERRED and are not fabricated or silently marked complete. They no longer block formal database identification under v2.1, but they remain required administrative actions before the review is represented as publicly registered and must be reported transparently.

Formal Stage 3 may begin only after (i) the current protocol is publicly accessible through a legitimate project-specific route, (ii) the updated fail-closed preflight passes and (iii) the user-authorized Stage 3 execution condition is recorded. Public protocol availability is distinct from OSF registration metadata and is not satisfied by the local D-drive copy. HG-04 remains a hard human screening gate: no 25-record pilot, title/abstract screening or full-text screening begins until a second real human reviewer is available.
